Dragon Card – Game Summary

Components:

  • 10 unique cards, each featuring two numbers (ability and health) and a special skill.

Dragon Assembly:

Each player builds a dragon by placing three cards into designated slots:

  • Head: Determines attack power.
  • Body: Determines defense value.
  • Tail: Determines speed and comes with a one-time-use special skill that becomes inactive after use.

The card’s health is equal to its ability value. When a part’s health drops to 0 or below, it is destroyed and discarded until replaced.

Setup Phase:

  • Player A: Draws 5 cards and selects 3 to form a red dragon (assigning them to head, body, and tail slots).
  • Player B: Uses the remaining 5 cards to form a blue dragon similarly.

The leftover cards are shuffled back into the deck.

Gameplay (Per Round):

Draw Opportunities: Each player starts with 2 draw chances per round. A draw lets a player replace one of their dragon’s parts by drawing a new card.

1. Action Phase:

  • Players may choose to activate their tail card’s special skill.
  • Each player selects one of their own parts to defend and one opponent’s part to attack.

Battle Resolution:

  • Turn Order: Determined by comparing the tail cards’ speed values; the higher speed acts first.

2. Attack Mechanics:

  • The attacker’s head card value is used as attack power.
  • If the target is defended, damage equals (attack power minus the target’s defense value), with a minimum of 0.
  • If not defended, the full attack power is applied as damage.
  • Damage reduces the target part’s health; if health falls to 0 or below, that part is destroyed.

3. Win Condition:

  • If a part is destroyed and its owner has exhausted their 2 draw chances, that player loses the game.

Rounds:

The game continues with subsequent rounds following the same structure until one player wins the game.
